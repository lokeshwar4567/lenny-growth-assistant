Topic: Product Growth

A strong growth process starts with understanding the customer problem.
Teams should talk to customers regularly, identify repeated pain points,
and prioritize problems that create meaningful value.

Topic: Product Strategy

A product strategy should clearly explain who the product serves,
what problem it solves, and why the proposed solution is valuable.
Good teams use evidence rather than assumptions when making decisions.

Topic: Experimentation

Growth experiments should have a clear hypothesis, measurable outcome,
and defined success criteria. Teams should learn from unsuccessful
experiments instead of treating them only as failures.
